10 Jack-o'-Lantern Stencils

Simple carving patterns you can sketch straight onto your pumpkin.

  1. #1

    Classic Scary Face

    Triangle eyes and nose with a jagged, tooth-lined grin — the original jack-o'-lantern.

  2. #2

    Silly Grin

    Round eyes, a round nose, and a wide toothy smile for a friendlier pumpkin.

  3. #3

    Black Cat Silhouette

    A sitting cat shape with pointed ears and a curled tail, carved right into the body.

  4. #4

    Bat Swarm

    Three small bats scattered across the pumpkin for a fluttering nighttime scene.

  5. #5

    Ghost

    A classic floating-sheet ghost with a wavy hem and two round eyes.

  6. #6

    Witch Hat & Moon

    A crescent moon paired with a classic pointed witch hat and brim.

  7. #7

    Spider & Web

    A radial web pattern with a spider perched right in the center.

  8. #8

    Owl

    Big round eyes, pointed ear tufts, and a small triangular beak.

  9. #9

    Haunted House

    A simple gabled house silhouette with a chimney and glowing cutout windows.

  10. #10

    Spiral Swirl Face

    Hypnotic spiral eyes with a wavy swirl mouth for a dizzy, cartoonish look.
