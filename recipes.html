10 Halloween-Themed Treats

Easy party food and snacks that look as fun as they taste.

  1. #1

    Mummy Hot Dogs

    Wrap hot dogs in strips of crescent roll dough, bake, and add mustard dot eyes.

  2. #2

    Spider Deviled Eggs

    Classic deviled eggs topped with olive-slice spider legs and a peppercorn body.

  3. #3

    Candy Corn Bark

    White chocolate bark studded with candy corn and sprinkles — a five-minute treat.

  4. #4

    Witch Finger Cookies

    Almond-tipped shortbread fingers with a red jam "cut" near the knuckle for effect.

  5. #5

    Monster Guacamole

    A bowl of guacamole with olive-slice eyes and a tortilla-chip "mouth" arranged on top.

  6. #6

    Pumpkin Patch Cupcakes

    Orange frosting swirled into a pumpkin shape, finished with a small pretzel stem.

  7. #7

    Eyeball Caprese Skewers

    Mozzarella balls with an olive "pupil" and a basil leaf, skewered with a cherry tomato.

  8. #8

    Graveyard Pudding Cups

    Chocolate pudding, crushed cookie "dirt," and a cookie tombstone stuck on top.

  9. #9

    Frankenstein Kiwis

    Kiwi halves with the peel left on, decorated with candy eyes and a mouth for a fruity monster face.

  10. #10

    Boo-nana Ghosts

    Frozen banana halves dipped in white chocolate with mini chocolate chip eyes.
